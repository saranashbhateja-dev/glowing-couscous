Jee OS — Start-date scheduling fix

- Preserve today's assigned lecture slots across refresh and cloud progress reload.
- Tie the daily lecture cache to the calendar date and configured plan start date.
- Changing Plan Start Date invalidates the cached daily allocation and recalculates the lecture/test timeline from the new start date.
- Existing lecture completion timestamps and historical test results are preserved.
- Revision due dates for completed chapters remain completion-based (completion date + existing revision intervals); they are not falsified or shifted as if a chapter were completed on another date.
